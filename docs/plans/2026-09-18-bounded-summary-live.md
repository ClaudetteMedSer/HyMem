### Retained cases: descriptive source review, not post-hoc accuracy

The four retained packets were independently reviewed against their full
authorized evidence before reviewer disclosure of live verdicts. The clearest
defect is granular travel episode zero: its sole cited visible span supplies
the horseback-wish suffix, but not the completed Big Sur trip or Santa Ynez
location asserted in its body/title. The context may finish the split word, not
independently authorize those facts. **Both verifier arms accept that item**
(027/028). A broad summary scope or another item's citation cannot repair it.

Three other boundaries remain descriptive: compressing explicit trip order to
an ordered list of destinations may weaken selected temporal meaning; the music
summary's `sought` language can obscure supplied recommendations; and grouping
the prior examples can leave category relationships ambiguous. These are not
new binary gold labels. Travel granular summary's sentence-plus-fragment is a
separate format issue, outside these direct fidelity calls. Bounded rejects the
travel blob summary in 026, but a different scope's veto is not detection of
the granular item's unsupported claims.

### Decision and follow-up

**Do not promote the treatment or clear LME.** Keep default legacy behavior,
strict failed-work reporting, canonical source guards and all old evidence
unchanged. The changed compression contract addresses one capacity conflict,
but neither verifier accuracy nor full end-to-end convergence is established.

Earlier isolated-verifier and reasoning-mode experiments already failed their
quality gates. Before another prompt rewrite, prepare a separate bounded
Flash-versus-Pro verifier comparison on these same 16 direct packets, using
only the bounded policy and changing only the requested model. This is a
diagnostic experiment under standing test permission, not a runtime model change
or a reroll of this campaign. The
[completed model comparison](2026-09-18-verifier-model-comparison.md) likewise
failed its quality gate: Flash matched 9/12 fixed controls and Pro 8/12, with
three and four false vetoes respectively. Pro did catch the retained citation
defect. Neither model is promoted, and LME remains uncleared.
